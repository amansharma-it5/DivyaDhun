## Full description

Make space for daily devotion with DivyaDhun, a simple devotional audio companion for prayer, reflection, and peaceful listening.

Explore bhajans, aartis, mantras, chalisa, stotra, and meditation content across beloved deities. Search the catalog, browse by deity or category, and start listening with clear playback controls.

Features:

- Stream the current verified devotional catalog over HTTPS.
- Browse Home, Explore, deity pages, categories, and Search.
- Play, pause, seek, queue, and move between tracks.
- Continue playback in the background with Android media controls.
- Save favorites and revisit recent listening locally on your device.
- Set an optional Daily Bhakti reminder.
- Choose English or Hindi labels, theme, and simple mode.
- Create an optional local Family Mandir profile.
- Share a blessing card through Android’s Sharesheet when you choose.

DivyaDhun does not require an account and does not include ads, subscriptions, or social posting. Network access is used to load the catalog and requested audio. Favorites, history, preferences, and the optional Family Mandir profile remain on your device.
